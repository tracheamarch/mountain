Hybrid Mountain Rules

  • Boxing Integration: Leave at least 6–8 hours (or separate days) between Title HIIT Boxing and heavy Upper/Lower lifting when possible.
  • Compound Lifts: Stop with 1–2 Reps in Reserve (RPE 8–9) to build mass without frying your CNS.
  • Isolation & Machine Lifts: Push the final set to true muscular failure (RPE 10).
  • Ski Eccentrics: Lower every weight for a controlled 2–4 seconds; never bounce out of the bottom of squats or calf raises.
  • Auto-Archive: Completing all sets archives your numbers. Your next session automatically clears the boxes while showing last week's numbers in grey.

8-Week Macro-Cycle

Switching weeks automatically resets completed workouts and loads your previous numbers as greyed-out reference text.

Weeks 1–4 (Accumulation): Master eccentric control, hit top of rep ranges.
Week 5 (Deload): Drop 1 set per exercise, keep RPE at 6–7 for joint/CNS recovery.
Weeks 6–8 (Intensification): Progressive overload on compounds + drop sets on machines.
